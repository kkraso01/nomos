# Research Sources Checked — 23 Sep 2026

These URLs were checked while preparing the handoff. Re-check before production source activation.

1. CyLaw Terms of Use  
   https://www.cylaw.org/terms.html  
   Key point: without prior written consent, terms prohibit external indexing by web robots and mass/systematic downloading; other services are directed to original/approved sources.

2. Cyprus National Open Data Portal FAQ  
   https://www.data.gov.cy/en/faq  
   Key point: portal says datasets are generally available under CC BY 4.0, permitting reproduction/modification with attribution and identification of changes. Dataset-specific terms still need preservation.

3. Cyprus Government Gazette service (Gov.cy)  
   https://www.gov.cy/en/service/episimi-efimerida-tis-dimokratias/  
   Key point: official Government Printing Office service for searching Gazette issues/annexes including legislative acts. Bulk-reuse permission is not inferred merely from official/public status.

4. Cyprus Supreme Court / Judicial Service decisions  
   https://www.supremecourt.gov.cy/  
   Key point: official source publishes final decisions. Source-specific automation/reuse conditions must be verified before systematic harvesting.

5. EUR-Lex reuse guidance  
   https://eur-lex.europa.eu/content/help/data-reuse/reuse-contents-eurlex-details.html?locale=en  
   Key point: data may be reused free of charge subject to copyright conditions; official webservice, data dumps, CELLAR REST/SPARQL and machine-readable formats are available. Large-volume users should use mass-data mechanisms.

6. HUDOC / ECHR official case-law database  
   https://www.echr.coe.int/hudoc-database  
   https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/ENG  
   Key point: official ECHR database covers judgments, decisions, communicated cases, advisory opinions and legal summaries. This handoff keeps bulk commercial reuse status gated until exact reuse/automation terms are verified.
