# Eval Fixtures

All corpus documents under this directory are **SYNTHETIC / TEST-ONLY** (see
`nomos_cy_corpus.json` header). They are not real, citable legal authorities and
must never be presented as authoritative legal data. They exist solely to make the
retrieval benchmark reproducible.

Hard negatives in `eval/expected/` resemble relevant material but are legally or
factually inappropriate and must remain retrievable-but-irrelevant.
